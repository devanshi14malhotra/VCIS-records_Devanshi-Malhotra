01 About Synergy

Vision & Mission

Synergy – the Sports Society of Indira Gandhi Delhi Technical University for Women (IGDTUW) – is the institutional home of athletic excellence, team spirit, and competitive ambition. The society's mission is to provide every IGDTUW student with structured sports training, inter-university competitive opportunities, and the platform to represent the institution with pride at all levels of competition.

Role of Sports in Holistic Development

At IGDTUW, Synergy ensures that students are equipped not only with academic credentials but with the competitive instincts, mental fortitude, and collaborative skills that modern professional life demands. The rigour of training, the pressure of competition, and the maturity that comes from both victory and defeat are lessons that no classroom can fully replicate.

Contribution to Campus Culture

Synergy is the beating heart of IGDTUW's extracurricular life. Through intra-college tournaments, selection trials, coaching sessions, and representation at premier inter-university festivals, the society has built a culture where sport is discipline – not merely recreation. The society's open selection policy ensures students from every department and academic year are welcome to compete.

Commitment to Excellence: 2025-2026 Session

In the 2025 competition season, Synergy represented IGDTUW at over 20 events across 10+ premier institutions including IIT Kanpur, IIT Delhi, IIT Roorkee, DTU, LNMIIT, ADGIPS, Shaheed Rajguru College, Symbiosis, and Shiv Nadar University. The society fielded teams in Basketball, Football, Volleyball, Athletics, Badminton, Chess, Kho-Kho, Table Tennis, and Taekwondo.

Key Highlights: 2025-2026

The following milestones define Synergy's competitive identity and growth in the 2025-2026 season:

Basketball – Double Podium

IGDTUW Basketball secured Silver & Bronze at Astitva'25 (ADGIPS) and followed with a Silver at Udghosh'25 (IIT Kanpur), making it the team's strongest multi-event season on record.

Badminton – Three-Festival Run

Three consecutive inter-university appearances at LNMIIT, IIT Delhi, and DTU – including two semi-finals and a Silver – confirm Badminton as one of Synergy's most consistent podium sports.

Chess – Gold at Spardhaa'25

Tripti Bansal and Prathivya Gupta claimed 1st position at Spardhaa'25, while the chess squad simultaneously competed at IIT Kanpur, DTU, and IIT Delhi – a landmark multi-front season.

Taekwondo – IIT Roorkee Gold + Best Player

At Sangram (IIT Roorkee), IGDTUW's Taekwondo athlete claimed Gold and the Best Player Award. Combined with national 5th-rank finishes at Khelo India and TFI Nationals, this represents IGDTUW's strongest individual sporting campaign.

10

Football – First Udghosh Podium

The Football team's Bronze at Udghosh'25 (IIT Kanpur) is a historic first – IGDTUW's first recorded medal at this prestigious festival in football, signalling the sport's rapid development within Synergy.

Athletics – 9 Events, 1 Festival

At Aahvan'25, IGDTUW athletes competed across nine separate athletics events from sprints to field events. The 4×400m Relay and Shotput teams earned 3rd place, reflecting strong depth across the squad.

Broadest Institutional Reach

2025 saw IGDTUW compete at IIT Kanpur, IIT Delhi, IIT Roorkee, DTU, LNMIIT, ADGIPS, Shaheed Rajguru College, Symbiosis, Shiv Nadar, and more – the widest inter-institutional sports footprint in the society's recent history.

05 Events Organised by Synergy

Synergy's contribution to IGDTUW extends beyond external competition. The society actively shapes the campus sports calendar through internally organised events and year-round athletic development initiatives.

Intra-College Sports Tournaments

Annual intra-college tournaments are organised across all active disciplines at the start of each semester. These serve as selection platforms and standalone competitive events, creating a vibrant in-campus sports culture accessible to the entire student body.

Selection Trials & Team Formation

Open, merit-based selection trials are held each semester. Students from all years and departments are invited to try out. This ensures Synergy's competitive squads always reflect the best available talent at IGDTUW, and no eligible student is excluded.

Training & Practice Sessions

Synergy maintains regular weekly training schedules for all major disciplines in coordination with college facilities. Coaches, senior athletes, and external specialists contribute to structured skill development that prepares teams for competition year-round.

Awareness & Wellness Workshops

Workshops on sports nutrition, injury prevention, warm-up protocols, and mental resilience are organised periodically for active athletes and open to the broader student community, reflecting the society's commitment to sustainable, informed athletic practice.

Collaboration with Campus Bodies

Synergy collaborates with the NSS, Student Council, and Cultural Committee on joint events and campus-wide sports festivals, ensuring that the spirit of athletic excellence permeates every dimension of life at IGDTUW.

06 Final Thoughts

The 2025 competition season reaffirms what Synergy has always stood for: the belief that every student of IGDTUW deserves the opportunity to compete, grow, and represent her institution with excellence. This report documents not just medals and podiums, but the collective effort of over 150 student athletes who trained with discipline, competed with courage, and carried the name of IGDTUW to institutions across India.

The Basketball team's double podium and Silver at IIT Kanpur; the Badminton team's three-festival podium run; the Taekwondo athlete's Gold and Best Player Award at IIT Roorkee; the Chess team's 1st position at Spardhaa'25; the Football team's historic debut podium at Udghosh'25 – each of these moments belongs to the entire Synergy community.

As Academic Year 2025–26 continues, Synergy is committed to building on this foundation. New tournaments will be entered, new sports will be explored, and training infrastructure will be further strengthened. The vision is clear: to establish IGDTUW as one of the foremost women's sports institutions among technical universities in India.

The society thanks the university administration, faculty coordinators, coaches, and the families of every athlete for their support throughout the year. And to every student who wore the IGDTUW colours in 2025 – your effort, your resilience, and your excellence have made this report possible.
